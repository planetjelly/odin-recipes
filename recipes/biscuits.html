Biscuits

Description

I love homemade biscuits! They are soft, fluffy, and make for an amazing breakfast side. Personally, I always pair my biscuits with homemade sausage gravy!

This recipe requires you to freeze the butter ahead of time, at least 30 minutes. If it is particularly warm in your kitchen, you may consider chilling the dough for 15-30 minutes after step 7. The cold butter is essential for getting that classic rise and fluff that we are going for.

Equipment

  • Box grater
  • Biscuit cutter
  • Mixing Bowls
  • Parchment Paper

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter, frozen
  • 3/4 cup buttermilk

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425F and line a cookie sheet with parchment paper
  2. Combine flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt into a large bowl and mix well. Set aside.
  3. Remove butter from freezer ans use box grater to shred butter into small pieces. Add shredded butter to the flour mixture.
  4. Combine grated butter until the mixture resembles a coarse mixture.
  5. Add buttermilk and mix until just combined, don't over work the dough.
  6. Transfer your biscuit dough to a well floured surface and use your hands to gently work the dough together. If the dough is too sticky, add flour 1 tbsp at a time until managable.
  7. Once dough is cohesive, fold in halfover itself and use your hands to gently flatten layers together. Rotate the dough 90 degrees and fold in half again, repeating this step 5-6 times but taking care to not overwork the dough.
  8. Use your hands to flatten the dough to 1 inch thick and lightly dust a 2 3/4 inch round biscuit cutter with flour.
  9. Making close cuts, press the biscuit onto your prepared baking sheet.
  10. Repeat until you have as many biscuits as possible and place less that 1/2 inch apart on the baking sheet.
  11. Once you have gotten as many biscuits as possible out of the fough, gently rework the dough to get another bicuit or two until you have 6 biscuits.
  12. Bake on 425F for 12 minutes or until tops are beginning to turn lightly golden brown.
  13. Brush with melted butter immediately after removing from oven.

Notes

This pairs fantastic with sausage gravy!
